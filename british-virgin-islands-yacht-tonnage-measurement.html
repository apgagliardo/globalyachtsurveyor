British Virgin Islands Yacht Tonnage Measurement

Tony Gagliardo is an IIMS-authorized tonnage measurer for applicable British Virgin Islands registration assignments involving vessels under 24 metres load line length. He works directly with yacht owners, brokers and registration agents to coordinate the measurement and required documentation.

Request a BVI tonnage quotation

BVI tonnage measurement and registration process

A Certificate of Measurement or Certificate of Survey (Tonnage Measurement) may be required for a qualifying vessel being registered in the British Virgin Islands. The applicable route depends on the vessel type, length, intended registration and current Registry requirements. The Registry’s simplified Part III route applies to qualifying non-fishing vessels under 24 metres; vessels outside that scope may be subject to different tonnage rules and authorization requirements.

For the qualifying under-24-metre route, the prescribed calculation uses defined vessel dimensions, including overall length, extreme breadth and depth, with any applicable breaks assessed under the regulations. The official Certificate of Survey form records vessel particulars and gross and net tonnage. Tony confirms the applicable measurement scope and accepted paperwork with the client and registration agent before an assignment proceeds.

IIMS authorization and Registry acceptance

IIMS identifies BVI tonnage measurement for vessels under 24 metres load line length as a scope it processes through its authorized measurers. Tony conducts the applicable measurement and prepares the required information through that route. IIMS performs its certifying-authority role where applicable; the Virgin Islands Shipping Registry determines whether submitted documents are accepted and handles the registration decision. Tony does not issue a BVI Certificate of Registry.

For vessels at or above the applicable length limit, fishing vessels or other cases outside this route, confirm the accepted measurer and requirements directly with the Registry or registration agent. This page does not claim IIMS authorization for those cases.

What to send with your enquiry

Send the builder, model, year, principal dimensions, current location, existing registry and intended BVI registration. Include any existing tonnage certificate, the Registry form or instructions from your registration agent, and available general arrangement plans. Photographs or additional plans may be needed for some verification routes; Tony will confirm what applies to your vessel.

Owners, brokers and closing agents can include the target registration or transaction date. Tony will review the scope, access and documentation, then provide an individual quotation and expected schedule. No remote-only measurement eligibility is assumed.

Direct support for owners, brokers and closing agents

International owners can coordinate directly with Tony, wherever the yacht is located. Survey arrangements and any attendance are considered against the vessel, applicable method, available documentation and Registry requirements. For established IIMS assignments, typical turnaround is 3–5 business days after the required information is complete and measurement has been carried out; Tony confirms the expected timing for each assignment.

Brokers and registration agents are welcome to share their client’s requirements and closing timetable so the scope, fee and practical arrangements can be agreed before work begins.

Frequently asked questions

When is a tonnage survey required for BVI yacht registration?

The Registry may require a Certificate of Measurement or Certificate of Survey (Tonnage Measurement) for the relevant registration route. Requirements depend on vessel type and length; confirm the applicable form and measurer with the Registry or your registration agent.

Is gross tonnage the yacht’s weight or displacement?

No. Tonnage is a regulatory measurement calculated under the rules for the intended registry. It is not the yacht’s physical weight or displacement.

What information is needed to arrange a measurement?

Provide vessel particulars and dimensions, its current location and registry, the intended BVI registration, and any existing certificate, Registry form, agent instructions or available plans. Include your target date.

Does the yacht need to be physically measured?

The measurer must establish the dimensions required by the applicable method. Whether attendance is needed depends on the vessel, method, records and Registry or certifying-authority requirements. Remote-only eligibility should not be assumed.

Who processes and accepts the documents?

The measurement and paperwork follow the applicable authorized-measurer and certifying-authority process. The Virgin Islands Shipping Registry decides whether the submission meets its requirements and makes the registration decision.
