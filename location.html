Locations & International Availability

Marine Surveying Across the Caribbean, Mediterranean & Beyond

Global Yacht Surveyor provides independent marine surveying and technical services to yacht owners, purchasers, brokers, and other marine industry professionals.

Our work has taken us throughout the Caribbean and Mediterranean, with assignments ranging from comprehensive onboard inspections to specialized technical consulting and yacht tonnage measurement.

Global Yacht Surveyor is an independent professional practice. Clients deal directly with Tony Gagliardo, whose surveying and engineering experience forms the basis of the services provided. Vessel inspections and surveying services are personally undertaken by Tony.

Vessel attendance and other assignments are considered individually according to location, scope, scheduling, and practical arrangements.

Southern Mediterranean

Over approximately the past two years, surveying activity has concentrated on the Southern Mediterranean, including Sicily, Southern Italy, Malta, Greece, and surrounding cruising areas.

Services include pre-purchase and insurance surveys, vessel appraisals, technical investigations, and consulting assignments.

Vessel attendance is arranged according to the location, schedule, and requirements of each assignment.

Caribbean

Global Yacht Surveyor has an established history of personally conducted yacht surveys throughout the Caribbean, including Trinidad and Tobago, Grenada, St. Lucia, and other islands.

This experience encompasses cruising yachts, marine systems, vessel condition assessments, and the challenges associated with vessels operating in tropical and offshore environments.

New Caribbean assignments may be considered by arrangement, subject to availability and travel requirements.

International Assignments

Our services are not limited to the Caribbean or Mediterranean.

I undertake marine surveying and technical consulting assignments internationally, with travel and inspection arrangements considered individually according to the vessel's location and the scope of work.

Certain assignments, including suitable vessel valuations, technical document reviews, and aspects of tonnage measurement, may also be completed without physical attendance where applicable requirements and available evidence permit.

Each inquiry is evaluated individually to determine the most appropriate approach.
