Firsthand knowledge

The perspective of
a Swan 46 owner.

As owner and operator of a Nautor’s Swan 46, I understand the care, preparation and attention to detail that a quality cruising yacht deserves.

Significant offshore sailing experience and professional client deliveries inform my approach. I bring the practical understanding of an owner together with the technical discipline of a naval architect and marine surveyor.

Before departure → Under way → At handover

More than moving the yacht.

A considered passage, followed by a clearer picture of how your particular boat works.

01 / PREPARE

Readiness first

Vessel familiarization, readiness review and passage planning establish what needs attention before departure. Route, weather, equipment and the practical demands of the passage shape the plan.

02 / DELIVER

Manage the passage

Engineering-led delivery combines offshore seamanship with close attention to the yacht’s systems in use. Equipment nuances and practical lessons are recorded along the way.

03 / HAND OVER

Keep the knowledge

A structured technical handover brings together vessel-specific operational notes, useful routines and points to discuss, giving you practical knowledge to refer to after arrival.

Knowledge you can use

Your yacht.
Its own operating story.

Even well-built yachts have their own systems, arrangements and habits. Time aboard reveals details that a generic manual cannot capture.

Vessel-specific operational notes

  • Equipment settings, sequences and operating nuances
  • Practical routines developed during the passage
  • Lessons learned from using the yacht and its systems
  • Context for the technical discussion at handover

These are operational notes from use, not a second marine survey or a promise of defect findings. Any marine survey is a separately agreed service.
