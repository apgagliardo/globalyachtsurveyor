Transport Canada–appointed tonnage measurement

Canadian Yacht Tonnage Measurement

Tony Gagliardo is a Transport Canada–appointed tonnage measurer for the 15–24 metre and over-24-metre vessel categories, within the terms of his appointment. He works with yacht owners, brokers and registration agents to assess the applicable measurement method, documentation and practical arrangements.

Canadian tonnage measurement by appointment

For Canadian vessel registration, tonnage is determined under the Vessel Registration and Tonnage Regulations and Transport Canada’s Standard for the Tonnage Measurement of Vessels (TP 13430). The applicable method and documentation depend on the vessel and its registration circumstances. Contact Tony with the vessel details and available Transport Canada or registry instructions to confirm whether the assignment falls within his appointment.

The appointment described here covers the 15–24 metre and over-24-metre categories. This page does not advertise appointed measurement below 15 metres. Length definitions can vary by method, so the applicable measurement basis and assignment scope are confirmed before a quotation is issued.

Simplified and appointed measurement methods

Some qualifying vessels may use an owner-completed Simplified Method under Part 3 of TP 13430. Published criteria include specified vessel and superstructure characteristics; separate provisions are available for certain simple multihulls. Eligibility depends on the full standard and the vessel’s particulars. Owners should confirm the applicable form and method with Transport Canada.

Other assignments require an appointed tonnage measurer. Tony reviews the applicable method and prepares the measurement and documents required within the appointment’s scope. Transport Canada remains responsible for its registration and acceptance decisions.

International assignments and practical arrangements

Canadian owners, brokers and registration agents may be coordinating a vessel located outside Canada. International assignments are considered individually according to vessel location, access, measurement method, schedule and available documents. A Canadian registration requirement does not by itself establish that a measurement can be completed remotely or without attendance.

For an initial review, provide the vessel’s builder, model, year, principal dimensions, current location and registration status, intended Canadian registration, available plans or existing tonnage certificates, and any Transport Canada or agent instructions. Include the target registration or transaction date. Tony will confirm scope, arrangements, timing and fee before work begins.

Questions from owners, brokers and agents

Can I enquire about an appointed Canadian measurement now?

Yes. Enquiries are welcome for vessels in the 15–24 metre and over-24-metre categories covered by Tony’s appointment. The method, measurement basis and scope are confirmed for each vessel before a quotation. This page does not advertise appointment below 15 metres.

Can an owner use the simplified method instead?

Some vessels may qualify for an owner-completed method under TP 13430. Eligibility depends on the full standard; the owner should confirm the correct form with Transport Canada.

Does the 24-metre threshold alone determine the required method?

No. Vessel characteristics, length definitions and registration circumstances also matter. Tony confirms the relevant scope and method against the appointment and current Transport Canada requirements.

What should an overseas owner send?

Send the vessel’s particulars, current location and registration details, intended Canadian registration, available plans or certificates, agent instructions and target dates. These details help assess access and practical arrangements.
